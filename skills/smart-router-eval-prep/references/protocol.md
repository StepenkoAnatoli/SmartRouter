# Frozen evaluation protocol

Preparation only. No trial has been performed by this document. Canonical routing/permission rules remain in [smart-router](../../smart-router/SKILL.md) and its contract.

## Arms and controls

A: qualified strong model directly. B: qualified economical model directly. C: preregistered simple fixed rule (economical for specific objectively checkable mechanical tasks, strong otherwise). D: SmartRouter with all overhead. Keep project workflow, acceptance, privacy and effects constant; inability to qualify a baseline is recorded, not silently replaced. No unsafe consequential access to make a baseline comparable.

Freeze exact task fixtures/versions, inputs, environments, host/model/catalog/price revisions, tools, cache/warm-start conditions, ordering/randomization, tuning/holdout split, repeats and grading. Include mechanical work, bounded features, ambiguity/architecture, conflicting research evidence, long context, required tools/vision, local-only restrictions, insufficient budgets and recovery. Use isolated nonproduction environments; blind qualitative grading where feasible and objective hidden checks where applicable.

Preregister a primary comparison to A and superiority/equivalence comparisons to C. B measures cheap-direct tradeoffs, not a deliberately weak straw baseline. Plan missing/blocked/cancelled handling and aggregate cost allocation before observing results. Analyze task categories and severe defects separately so refusal or narrow mechanical wins cannot imply universal savings.

## Required preregistration

Fill [the template](../assets/preregistration.md), with approved numeric values for:

- Maximum acceptance/quality degradation versus A and category-specific mandatory floors.
- Minimum meaningful cost-per-success benefit, maximum latency/reviewer/operator-effort regressions, and D-vs-C equivalence margins.
- Repetitions per task/arm, coverage/sample minimum, randomization and uncertainty interval method/confidence level; decision on interval crossing thresholds.
- Zero severe authorization/privacy/data-loss/unsafe-replay failures for promotion; immediate stop and investigation for any such event. Narrow severe definitions must not hide permission breaches.
- Required accounting completeness and bounded uncertainty treatment; missing unresolved billable usage prevents a savings promotion. No invented replacement charges.
- Training/tuning exclusions, holdout, model drift, deadline, maximum cost/attempts and permitted recovery.

No numeric threshold here is a universal default. Blank values block readiness. Register the signed-off full protocol revision before trials; changes require a new protocol and separate results, not retrofitted pass criteria. Obtain explicit spend/data/environment authorization after preparation.

## Measures and ledger

Use [the ledger](../assets/trial-ledger.md). Report task/attempt counts, accepted/unverified/failed/blocked/cancelled, severe defects, unsupported claims, check truthfulness, total accounted charges, missing/uncertain charges, cost per verified successful task, retries/escalations, elapsed latency/context and review/operator effort. Report cash, quota, resources and effort separately.

Sum all billed attempts and verification, including routing/classification, framing, failed first attempts, cancelled/timeouts, re-priming, retries/repair and tools. Estimates never replace observed totals. If later an expected-cost model uses an escalation aggregate, state whether earlier sunk costs are included. Predicted success must be calibrated for the task distribution, not imported tier confidence.

Zero accepted tasks -> cost/success **undefined** and arm cannot satisfy promotion. Accounting gaps -> incomplete/unknown economic result. A block with no call is recorded as a block, not a successful zero-cost task. Bootstrapped or other uncertainty methods must match repeated/paired task structure; do not treat dependent repeats as independent samples without justification.

## Decision rule (apply in order)

1. **Reject / stop unsafe candidate:** any severe safety failure; systematically unqualified routes; bypassed authority/gates; or sufficiently evidenced failure of preregistered quality/cost/latency/effort bounds. Investigation/correction is mandatory before a new candidate/protocol; disclosure is no waiver. Missing required checks block promotion even if not severe.
2. **Inconclusive:** absent/ambiguous accounting, zero successful tasks in a required comparison, insufficient coverage/repetitions, drifted or unregistered protocol, uncertainty crossing acceptance/benefit thresholds, or unsupported comparability. Report what additional evidence is needed; never claim savings.
3. **Simplify:** with complete evidence and hard gates met, C or eligible direct work meets all required quality/benefit constraints and D has no meaningful incremental benefit under frozen equivalence margins. Retain the simpler safe policy; do not ship a selector to justify sunk effort.
4. **Promote narrowly:** D passes all hard gates and required holdout checks, acceptance/quality noninferiority and mandatory floors are supported, cost benefit exceeds the preregistered minimum with the required uncertainty support, latency/effort limits pass, and meaningful benefit over safe simpler alternatives is supported. State exact workload/versions/limitations. Promotion permits a recommendation for separately approved adoption, not an automatic install/merge.

An exploratory pilot never proves universal same-quality savings. Publish negative and inconclusive results. Mechanical-only gains may justify a mechanical-only policy; broader consumer rollout needs separately reviewed evidence.
